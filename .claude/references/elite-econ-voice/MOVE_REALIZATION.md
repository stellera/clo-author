# Move Realization

This file maps rhetorical jobs to preferred linguistic realizations. It does not change the Scientific Style Bundle's permissions.

## PHENOMENON / PUZZLE
Prefer:
- concrete object first;
- a visible contrast, anomaly, reversal, or unresolved relationship;
- one sentence that establishes why the object is economically consequential.

Avoid:
- generic importance claims;
- broad historical throat-clearing;
- "There is a large literature on..." as the opening move.

Synthetic realization:
> Firms facing the same national policy often adjust very differently across places, raising the question of which local constraints shape the response.

## UNKNOWN / RESEARCH_QUESTION
Prefer:
- "We study whether/how..."
- direct unresolved contrast;
- a question tied to an observable object.

Avoid:
- "The purpose of this paper is..."
- topic-only formulations with no unresolved margin.

Synthetic realization:
> We study whether access to alternative suppliers changes how firms absorb an external trade shock.

## SETTING
Prefer:
- institution/event/rule first;
- date, threshold, eligibility rule, or coverage when available;
- explain why the setting creates a useful comparison.

Synthetic realization:
> The reform applied to firms above a statutory threshold, creating a discrete change in exposure at the cutoff.

## DATA
Prefer:
- source → unit → coverage → constructed object;
- active research verbs: use, link, construct, merge, geocode.

Synthetic realization:
> We link customs records to firm ownership files and construct annual measures of supplier concentration at the firm-product level.

## MEASUREMENT
Prefer:
- state the object being measured;
- explain the construction choice;
- report the validation target before interpretation.

Synthetic realization:
> We measure local exposure as the pre-reform share of inputs sourced from affected origins and validate the index against observed shipment disruptions.

## IDENTIFICATION
Prefer:
- name the source of variation early;
- connect the variation directly to the estimand;
- distinguish what varies from what must hold.

Synthetic realization:
> We exploit the staggered introduction of the rule across product categories and compare more- and less-exposed firms within industry-year cells.

## IDENTIFYING_ASSUMPTION
Prefer:
- plain counterfactual statement;
- observable implication or check immediately after.

Synthetic realization:
> The design requires that, absent the reform, high- and low-exposure firms would have followed similar outcome paths. We assess this implication using pre-reform event-study coefficients.

## IDENTIFICATION_THREAT
Prefer:
- threat named precisely;
- consequence for the estimate stated;
- adjacent response/check.

Synthetic realization:
> A remaining concern is that exposed firms were already reorienting suppliers. Pre-reform sourcing trends do not show such differential adjustment.

## SPECIFICATION
Prefer:
- estimator only after the comparison is clear;
- equation prose names absorbed variation rather than listing fixed effects mechanically.

Synthetic realization:
> The specification compares firms with different pre-reform exposure within the same industry and year while absorbing time-invariant firm differences.

## MAIN_RESULT
Prefer:
- result object first;
- one quantity;
- one immediately interpretable comparison.

Synthetic realization:
> Exposure reduced import growth by 6 percent, equivalent to roughly one-third of the pre-reform cross-firm standard deviation.

## MAGNITUDE
Prefer:
- comparator with economic meaning;
- short translation sentence if the main result sentence is already dense.

Synthetic realization:
> Relative to the baseline mean, the estimate corresponds to a decline of about 12 percent.

## NULL_RESULT
Prefer:
- "we find no evidence...";
- precision/bound if possible;
- do not turn a null into positive disproof.

Synthetic realization:
> We find no detectable change in domestic employment, and the confidence interval rules out effects larger than 2 percent.

## HETEROGENEITY
Prefer:
- subgroup/gradient as a contrast;
- keep interpretation separate.

Synthetic realization:
> The decline is concentrated among firms with few pre-existing alternative suppliers.

## MECHANISM
Prefer:
- discriminating prediction first;
- test/readout second;
- attribution at licensed strength.

Synthetic realization:
> If supplier substitution is the relevant margin, effects should be weaker where alternative origins were already available. The estimates follow this gradient, which is consistent with substitution capacity shaping adjustment.

## ROBUSTNESS
Prefer:
- perturbation → readout → scope verdict.

Synthetic realization:
> Re-estimating the model with product-by-year fixed effects leaves the coefficient essentially unchanged. This makes differential product-level shocks an unlikely explanation for the baseline pattern.

## LITERATURE_POSITION
Prefer:
- strand capability;
- unresolved margin;
- precise delta in evidence/design.

Synthetic realization:
> Existing work shows that trade shocks alter firm outcomes, but it does not identify whether pre-existing supplier options mediate adjustment. Our design isolates that margin directly.

## CONTRIBUTION
Prefer:
- contribution as a change in what can be learned, not an adjective.

Synthetic realization:
> The contribution is to separate exposure to the shock from the capacity to reallocate across suppliers.

## LIMITATION
Prefer:
- exact boundary;
- what evidence would lift it.

Synthetic realization:
> The design identifies short-run adjustment among incumbent firms; evaluating entry responses would require establishment-level creation data.

## GENERALIZATION
Prefer:
- named extrapolation target;
- explicit warrant.

Synthetic realization:
> The mechanism should extend most directly to settings in which inputs are standardized and alternative suppliers are observable before the shock.
