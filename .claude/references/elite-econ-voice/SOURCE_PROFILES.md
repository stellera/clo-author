# Source-Stratum Profiles

These profiles are provenance notes, not imitation prompts.

## Daron Acemoglu stratum
Most useful for:
- large-question framing;
- explicit rival explanations;
- theory/empirics linkage;
- identifying assumptions stated as substantive objects.

Do not imitate:
- distinctive historical/political-economy phrasing;
- topic-specific vocabulary.

## Raj Chetty stratum
Most useful for:
- enumeration of empirical facts;
- transparent magnitude translation;
- descriptive/causal separation;
- baseline and counterfactual comparisons.

Do not imitate:
- project-specific terminology or branded measurement objects.

## Hanming Fang stratum
Most useful for:
- theory → prediction → evidence bridges;
- compact institutional exposition;
- mechanism and welfare comparisons.

## Nancy Qian stratum
Most useful for:
- puzzle/reversal framing;
- historical fact → identification transition;
- multi-step causal argument made explicit.

## Shaoda Wang stratum
Most useful for:
- institution → design → quantified result;
- concise China-focused applied-micro exposition;
- mechanism language linked to observable administrative behavior.

## David Y. Yang stratum
Most useful for:
- question/contrast-driven political-economy framing;
- experimental and quasi-experimental design descriptions;
- explicit scope boundaries and theoretical implications.

## Ensemble use
No single stratum has authority. A Clo-Author drafting call should use `VOICE_SPEC.md`, not these profiles, unless the user explicitly asks to inspect provenance.
