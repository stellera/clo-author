# Elite Economics Voice Corpus Manifest

**Bundle:** elite-econ-voice v1.0.0  
**Corpus size:** 30 distinct papers  
**Design:** 6 focal authors × 5 papers; equal author weight (1/6 each), equal within-author paper weight (1/5)  
**Purpose:** distill transferable empirical-economics prose habits, not reproduce any author's distinctive wording.

## Weighting and de-duplication

- Each focal author receives total weight 1/6 regardless of publication count outside this corpus.
- Each paper receives weight 1/5 within its focal-author stratum.
- No paper appears in two focal-author strata. In particular, the shared Wang–Yang paper *Policy Experimentation in China* is intentionally excluded to avoid correlated double-weighting.
- Coauthored prose is treated as evidence about the **ensemble**, not proof of an individual author's private writing habits.
- A rule can enter the authoritative Voice Spec only when it survives cross-author comparison; single-author quirks remain descriptive notes at most.
