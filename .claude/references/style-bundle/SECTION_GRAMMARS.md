# Section Grammars

## introduction

### gap-led empirical trunk

`PHENOMENON|MOTIVATION → UNKNOWN|RESEARCH_QUESTION → SETTING|DATA → IDENTIFICATION → MAIN_RESULT|MAGNITUDE → MECHANISM → LITERATURE_POSITION|CONTRIBUTION → ROADMAP`

**Use when:** Reduc-form or quasi-experimental paper whose identification is a named design and whose results are previewed.

Observed in the majority of the 15 introductions; the mechanism slot is optional and the roadmap slot is a default, not a rule.

### positioning-led entry

`LITERATURE_POSITION → THEORY|UNKNOWN → CONTRIBUTION → DATA → MAIN_RESULT|NULL_RESULT → CONTRIBUTION → GENERALIZATION`

**Use when:** Papers entering a contested literature where the puzzle is that prior estimates disagree.

Optional variant: legitimate but contradicted by one batch's anti-pattern against literature-first openers.

### instrumental-event tour

`MOTIVATION → RESEARCH_QUESTION → SETTING → DATA → MEASUREMENT → MAIN_RESULT → ROBUSTNESS → MECHANISM → CONTRIBUTION`

**Use when:** Design driven by an exogenous episode or rule change where the event itself must be established before it is used.

Seen in introduction batch 2 profiles (paper13, paper14, paper15).

## empirical_strategy

### model-then-variation

`SPECIFICATION → IDENTIFICATION → IDENTIFYING_ASSUMPTION → VALIDATION`

**Use when:** The estimating equation defines what the design can recover.

8-profile base; no readout moves permitted.

### assumption-defense chain

`IDENTIFICATION → IDENTIFICATION_THREAT → IDENTIFYING_ASSUMPTION → VALIDATION`

**Use when:** The strongest version of the assumption is visibly fragile and the section's job is to show what each assumption costs.

Threat is always followed by a check or a stated route to one.

## results

### readout then translation

`MAIN_RESULT → MAGNITUDE`

**Use when:** A first effect needs an interpretable unit before anything else is claimed from it.

The most frequent two-move pattern in the corpus's results sections.

### respecify and reread

`SPECIFICATION → MAIN_RESULT → ROBUSTNESS`

**Use when:** The same claim needs a second reading under a different estimand, sample or fixed-effect structure.

SPECIFICATION is reopened rather than assumed carried over.

### channel-by-gradient

`HETEROGENEITY → MECHANISM`

**Use when:** A channel is argued through where the effect concentrates.

The gradient must be labelled as suggestive; a direct channel test is a different grammar.

## mechanism

### discriminating test

`LABEL_CHANNEL → STATE_DISCRIMINATING_PREDICTION → SPECIFY_CHANNEL_ESTIMAND → READ_TEST_OR_GRADIENT → BOUND_WITH_ALTERNATIVE`

**Use when:** The channel has its own testable contrast.

Architecture labels are paragraph-internal steps, not taxonomy moves.

### rival-inventory defence

`NAME_RIVAL_CHANNEL → CHARACTERIZE_RIVAL_SIGNATURE → TEST_OR_INVOKE_DESIGN_RULE → REPORT_NULL_OR_CONTRAST`

**Use when:** Rival explanations outnumber available direct tests.

Where a rival is only argued against, the claim stays associational.

## robustness

### bounded battery

`ANNOUNCE_CHECK_SET → STATE_WHAT_COULD_BREAK_THE_ESTIMATE → PERTURB_SPECIFICATION → READOUT_AT_MAIN_SCALE → SCOPE_VERDICT`

**Use when:** Standard perturbations are run as a set.

9 of 9 profiles open on the apparatus, not the finding.

### contamination concession

`NAME_ERROR_SOURCE → CONCEDE_THE_UNTESTABLE_CASE → ARGUE_ORDER_OF_MAGNITUDE_OR_CONSERVATISM`

**Use when:** A specific measurement error or untestable assumption is the live threat.

Conceding the untestable case is legitimate here; inventing a new claim is not.

## data

### source then measure then check

`DATA → MEASUREMENT → VALIDATION`

**Use when:** A self-constructed or imputed variable must be shown to behave before it is used.

Runs at the corpus's lowest hedge floor with zero strong-claim verbs.

### intra-section switch

`ROADMAP → DATA → DATA`

**Use when:** The packet has a separate strategy section and the data section is long.

ROADMAP marks a change of object, never a findings preview.

## institutional_background

### rule-to-variation

`SETTING → MEASUREMENT → IDENTIFICATION → DATA`

**Use when:** The institution is the source of identifying variation, so each rule is stated with its date, threshold or coverage and then converted into the object it supplies.

Move sequence uses the taxonomy; the quantification requirement lives in HARD-BKGD-01, and the closing handoff is a DATA or MEASUREMENT paragraph rather than a summary.

## conclusion

### verdict then boundary

`RESTATE_ANSWER_OR_CONTRIBUTION → TRANSLATE_HEADLINE_MAGNITUDE → STATE_LIMITATION_AS_SCOPE → GENERALIZE_WITH_WARRANT`

**Use when:** The paper closes on its own evidence class.

9 of 9 profiles separate verdict from scope.

### differentiation close

`BOOK_CONTRIBUTION_AGAINST_STRAND → DIFFERENTIATE_FROM_NEAREST_WORK → REOPEN_QUESTION_OR_HAND_OFF_AGENDA`

**Use when:** The contribution is a new measurement object or reconciliation.

Agenda hand-off is optional style.

## abstract

### compressed result arc

`NAME_PRACTICE_OR_EPISODE → ANCHOR_QUESTION_OR_STAKES → NAME_DEVICE → REPORT_VERDICT_WITH_ONE_MAGNITUDE`

**Use when:** Empirical paper with one headline verdict.

Base of 4 papers: a default, not a hard rule.

## literature

### strand-gap-entry

`SURVEY_STRAND → STATE_WHAT_STRAND_CAN_SUPPORT → NAME_GAP_OR_LOWER_BOUND → CLAIM_DESIGN_ADVANTAGE`

**Use when:** Positioning is done by strand capability rather than by citation count.

Base of 3 papers.
